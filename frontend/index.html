District rule

Administrative district counts come from the Government of India Local Government Directory. District grievance metrics are never derived by splitting State/UT totals.

Interpretation rule

CIVICLENS reports descriptive workload patterns. Project-defined metrics such as pressure index are exploratory and are not official government scores.
